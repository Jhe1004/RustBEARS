# Figure export and journal formatting

The [Systematic Biology author instructions](https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/pages/General_Instructions)
were checked on 15 November 2026. They specify common sans-serif fonts, labelled
axes and units, lowercase panel letters followed by a closing parenthesis, and
legibility at the printed column width. Lines should generally be near 1 pt and
at least 0.5 pt. The page prefers vector figures; its production section requests
TIFF, EPS or JPEG files at a minimum of 300 dpi.

The project exports editable PDF/SVG plus PNG and lossless LZW TIFF at 600 dpi.
Panel labels use `a)`, `b)` and so on. The chosen physical dimensions and 8-point
font floor are project design settings, not numerical journal limits inferred
from that page. A source script passing preflight is not an approved figure.

Once formal data exist, rerun alignment, PDF text and collision checks, inspect
each panel at final size, and verify the exported TIFF dimensions and resolution.
Retain captions outside the image and include source data, exact inputs and
rendering provenance. Accepted-manuscript production requirements must be checked
again when preparing the final submission files.
